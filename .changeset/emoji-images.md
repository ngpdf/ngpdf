Adds `providePdfEmoji`, which draws emoji in color as small inline images. Give it a URL template such as `'/emoji/{codepoints}.png'`, where `{codepoints}` becomes the emoji's code points in lower-case hex joined by `-`, without U+FE0F (`1f44d-1f3fd` for 👍🏽), or a function that returns a PNG or JPEG for each emoji, or `null` to leave it as text. Each emoji is 1 em square at the text's size, sits on the line like the letters around it, breaks lines as one character, takes `letter-spacing` and underlines, and works with `max-lines` and justified text. ZWJ sequences, skin tones, flags and keycaps are drawn as one image. `©`, `®`, `™` and digits stay text on their own: they become emoji with U+FE0F after them, digits also in a keycap (with or without U+FE0F), and pictographic characters also in a ZWJ sequence or with a skin tone. Each distinct emoji is loaded once per render, and at most 500. An emoji whose image is missing, fails to load or takes more than 10 seconds is drawn as text with a warning in development mode, so it never fails the render. ngpdf ships no emoji images and fetches nothing unless you add the provider. An emoji image is not text, so it cannot be copied or searched, and color emoji fonts are still not supported.
