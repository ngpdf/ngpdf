'@ngpdf/core': minor

Adds `VERSION`, the version of `@ngpdf/core` your app runs, as an Angular `Version` with `full`, `major`, `minor` and `patch` (the same shape as `VERSION` from `@angular/core`). Import it from `@ngpdf/core` to put the version in bug reports, support tools or your own `producer`. The default `producer` stays `ngpdf`, so the bytes of your PDFs don't change between releases.
